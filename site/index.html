The problem

Hundreds of photos. Never looked at together.

They're split across camera rolls and group chats, shot by the one friend who always holds the phone, and buried a week later.

Drag the prints around

01 · Start a roll

Start a roll. Share one code.

Name it and pick 24, 36, 48 or 72 shots. Send the roll code and friends join in a tap, all shooting on the same roll.

02 · Shoot

Shoot. No peeking.

Every roll has a limited number of shots, and each one you take counts down. Nothing shows until the last frame, so you stay in the moment.

fewer shots, more moment

Tap the camera to try our two films. Hold a print to save it.

03 · Make it together

Build the scrapbook together, live.

When the roll develops, open the shared book. Everyone adds photos, tape, stickers and doodles at the same time, and you watch each other's pages come together.
